💡 Book for more than one day if you can. Most Bangalore rental chains price by the day but drop the per-day rate sharply on weekly/monthly slabs — e.g. listed rates around ₹200–400/day for a single day fall closer to ₹100–150/day equivalent on a week-plus booking. Even though this trip is one night, ask the hub directly if a 2-day booking works out cheaper than a strict 24-hour slot — return times rarely line up exactly.
